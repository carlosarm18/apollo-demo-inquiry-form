How Apollo assigns leads to admissions reps

Everything below is configured by the school in Prospects > Lead Assignment. No code changes.

Rules

  • Selection method
    • Load balanced fewest open leads first
    • Round robin takes turns
    • Weighted round robin senior reps get more
    • Longest idle whoever waited longest
  • Working hours assign only to reps on the clock
  • After hours assign anyway, hold until opening, or send to a backup rep
  • Capacity stop at each rep's maximum, or allow over capacity
  • Open lead statuses which statuses count toward workload
  • Notifications email or in-app to the rep; admin alert if a lead can't be assigned

Reps

  • Who is a rep any employee marked Admissions Representative
  • Campuses each rep covers the campuses they're assigned to
  • Program categories which categories each rep may receive
  • Availability available or away, with an "away until" date
  • Schedule working days, hours and time zone
  • Weight share of leads in weighted round robin
  • Max open leads per-rep capacity

Programs, queue and log

  • Categories group programs, for example Healthcare and Skilled Trades
  • Programs each program belongs to one category
  • Unassigned leads no rep could take, with the reason, assigned by hand in one click
  • Log every assignment and reassignment: when, to whom, why
  • Reassign move a lead to another rep, with the reason recorded
